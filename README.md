# Negative Group Delay Circuits: A Trade-Off Driven Review

Seminar paper | Institute of Robust Power Semiconductor Systems (ILH), University of Stuttgart
**Author:** Alvi Islam

A review of state-of-the-art Negative Group Delay (NGD) circuits, examined through five recurring design trade-offs: bandwidth vs. flatness, NGD magnitude vs. insertion loss, stability vs. tunability, compactness vs. performance, and high-frequency integration. No single architecture wins on every axis, and hybrid designs look like the most promising direction.
